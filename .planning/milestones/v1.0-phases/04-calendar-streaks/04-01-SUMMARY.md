## Decisions Made

- Used `@riverpod` function with `(int year, int month)` params for `calendarMonthProvider` family — code-gen generates the family correctly with multiple primitive params (per RESEARCH Pattern 1)
- Used `async* yield*` in `streakProvider` to forward a `.map()`-transformed stream from `watchDailyTotalsInRange` — cleaner than nested subscription
- `FocusedMonth` uses `@Riverpod(keepAlive: true) class` Notifier pattern consistent with codebase's exclusive use of code-gen (no raw StateProvider anywhere)
- Manual `_monthName(int month)` helper for day summary date formatting to avoid adding `intl` as a dependency (per RESEARCH open question 2)
- `_toDateKey(DateTime)` private helper in both `stream_providers.dart` and `history_screen.dart` — intentional duplication rather than a shared utility since CONTEXT.md recommended inline helpers

- **Committed in:** `d415b7a` (auto-fix commit)

Note: The plan explicitly said "Do NOT add `collection` to pubspec.yaml" based on the assumption that importing a transitive dep was lint-safe. In practice, the `depend_on_referenced_packages` lint rule requires explicit declaration. The fix was necessary for `flutter analyze` to pass (a hard verification criterion).
