Migrate the GoRouter from flat routes to StatefulShellRoute.indexedStack with a bottom NavigationBar (per D-06, D-07), update History and Settings screens to "Coming soon" stubs, and add the percent_indicator dependency. This establishes the tab navigation shell that all HOME-01 through HOME-04 features render within.

</objective>
